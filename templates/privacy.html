Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, browsing history, or any data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the expense tracking features of Spendly — displaying your records, generating summaries, and enabling filters. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

You can delete your account at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for infrastructure (such as hosting). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

We do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-brokering services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information provided on the site. We are committed to addressing any privacy-related concerns promptly.
